# Case management safety requirements

- Keep cases, notes and activity private; never expose them through public APIs or search.
- Separate case-manager access from content, finance and volunteer roles.
- Record who viewed or changed a case in an audit trail.
- Avoid copying sensitive details into notifications, URLs or client-side logs.
- Define retention, correction, export and deletion procedures before production use.
- Require explicit consent and a documented process before a case can inform a public story or impact report.
