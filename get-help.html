Confidential support

You deserve support.

Our secure help-request pathway is being prepared. We will only ask for information genuinely needed to understand what support may fit.

Protect your privacy

Do not include details that could place you at risk if someone else can access your device. Consider using a safe email address or phone number.

When the secure form is ready

You will be able to choose your preferred contact method, describe the kind of support you need and tell us whether there is an immediate safety concern.

For now, email hello@beautifulyoumk.com only if it is safe for you to do so.

Emergency and crisis contacts will be published after the organisation confirms the locations it serves. This website is not an emergency service.
