Your safety matters

Care, choice and respect.

You deserve to be heard without pressure. You decide what to share and what support feels right for you.

When you contact us

Please share only information that feels safe to send. Use the Get Help form when it is safe to use your device. Your request is reviewed by authorised Beautiful You By M.K. administrators so they can respond.

This website is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted local support provider.

Photos and personal stories

Your dignity and privacy come first. A photograph, name or personal story should not be published without informed, documented consent from the person involved.
