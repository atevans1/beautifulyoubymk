# Confidential Help Request safety requirements

- Keep the form out of search indexes and analytics intended for ordinary visitors.
- Ask only for information needed for assessment; preferred name should be sufficient.
- Show a clear warning not to include details that could create risk if another person can access the device.
- Validate and rate-limit on the server; do not trust browser validation alone.
- Do not send full submissions to ordinary email inboxes or log bodies in application logs.
- Do not publish submissions as stories, testimonials or impact statistics automatically.
- Restrict records to authorised case-management roles and record access in an audit trail.
- Define retention, deletion and emergency escalation rules before accepting live submissions.
