Why we exist

What happened to you does not have to be the end of your story.

Our work

Support that meets you where you are.

Our programmes grow with the needs of the people and communities we serve. More programme information will be published as services are confirmed.

Makeover Sessions

A restorative experience for hard-working women, single mothers and young adults rebuilding confidence after hardship or abuse.

Emotional support

Compassionate listening, counselling referrals and space to heal.

Life rebuilding

Practical guidance for housing, education, work and independent living.

Skills & opportunity

Training, mentorship and pathways toward financial independence.
